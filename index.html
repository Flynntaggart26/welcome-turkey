🛂 Visa & Entry

e-Visa at evisa.gov.tr for many nationalities (check yours). Passport 6+ months validity. Keep e-Visa PDF offline.

💱 Money

Currency: Turkish Lira (TRY, ₺). Cards widely accepted in cities, cash for bazaars/buses. ATMs everywhere. Tipping: 5-10% if no service charge.

🕌 Etiquette

  • Shoes off in mosques, shoulders/knees covered (scarves provided).
  • Tea offered = hospitality, not sales.
  • Bargain in bazaars, not in malls.

🚨 Emergency

112 universal, 155 police, 112 ambulance. Tourist police: 153 (Istanbul). Save offline.

🚕 Transport

Istanbul: Istanbulkart for metro/tram/ferry. Intercity: buses (Metro Turizm, Pamukkale) and domestic flights (Pegasus/Turkish Airlines). Use BiTaksi for taxis.

🌦 Best seasons

Apr-Jun & Sep-Oct: mild, fewer crowds. Cappadocia balloons fly early morning, wind-dependent. Antalya beach Jun-Sep.
